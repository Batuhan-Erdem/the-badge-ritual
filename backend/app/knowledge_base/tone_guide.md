# Tone Guide — The Badge Ritual

The language of The Badge Ritual must be:
- poetic but clear
- calm
- intimate
- reflective
- symbolic
- cinematic
- historically aware
- emotionally serious
- gentle but not sentimental

Avoid:
- motivational clichés
- therapy-like diagnosis
- medical or psychological claims
- direct advice such as "you should"
- shallow historical facts
- overly dramatic fantasy language
- modern productivity language
- saying the user is healed

Use:
- threshold imagery
- weight imagery
- silence
- dust
- twilight
- old wood
- worn metal
- breath
- leaving something behind
- a door that does not answer, but listens

Preferred style:
"You have carried this badge for a long time. It may have once protected you. But not every protection is meant to become a prison."

Historical echo style:
"In 1973, another badge became too heavy to wear. Not because it had no meaning, but because meaning itself had become heavy."

Final release style:
"You may knock now. Not because everything is finished, but because you are no longer carrying it in the same way."
